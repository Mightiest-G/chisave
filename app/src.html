Store settings

Puts an icon on your home screen, works without internet.

Without a text service, deals go out from your own phone — tap a regular's name and your messaging app opens with the text ready. With the service set up, "Send to all by text service" sends today's deals to every regular in one tap. It needs a Twilio account (~$1.15/mo for a number, less than a cent a text), so it stays off until then. Fully automatic 9am sending needs the regulars list on a server — that's a step after the pilot.

Connecting the register needs a small helper (a Cloudflare Worker) — its web address goes here. The helper only swaps the sign-in code for a token; it never sees your sales. Without it, sales still come in from the daily log or a CSV file.
